Our Heritage

A Legacy Sealed
in Every Drop

Born in the heart of the Arabian Peninsula, Khulafa was founded on a single belief: that fragrance is a form of poetry. For over two decades, our master perfumers have sourced the world's rarest ingredients — oud from Assam, rose from Grasse, saffron from Kashmir.

Each formula is composed by hand, aged in cedar-lined chambers, and bottled in hand-blown glass. No shortcuts. No compromise. Only the eternal luxury that bears our name.
